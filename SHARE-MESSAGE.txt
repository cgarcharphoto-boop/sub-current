Here's the Subcurrent app prototype, built around Re:Turnal Nights: https://thedude0007.github.io/subcurrent-app-prototype/

Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app:
iPhone: open the link in Safari, tap Share (the square with the arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
Android: open the link in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen (or Install app).

Then open it from the Subcurrent icon. Tap Owner portal at the top to see the money side.
